# Test-day checklist

Verify G+ is taken online, on your own, in one sitting. Most problems on the day are practical ones you can prevent.

## The day before

- [ ] Read the invitation again: the deadline, the test name and any rules on calculators or devices.
- [ ] If you need an adjustment, such as extra time, ask the employer now. Adjustments are arranged before you start, not during.
- [ ] Check your device and browser work by opening one of [SHL's practice tests](https://www.shl.com/shldirect/en/practice-tests/).
- [ ] Charge your laptop and plan to keep it plugged in.
- [ ] Choose a time when you are alert and will not be interrupted for 45 minutes.

## One hour before

- [ ] Find a quiet room. Tell anyone at home not to disturb you.
- [ ] Put out a calculator, two pens and a few sheets of paper. SHL's support pages say you can use a calculator for numerical tests unless told otherwise, and that if the test shows an on-screen calculator, use that one.
- [ ] Close other tabs and apps, and turn off notifications.
- [ ] Have a glass of water nearby.

## In the first minute

- [ ] Read the instructions screen fully. It tells you the number of questions and the time limit for your version.
- [ ] Note where the timer is shown.
- [ ] Write the three method reminders at the top of your paper: "base value first", "must, not might", "every feature".

## During the test

- [ ] Read the question before the data.
- [ ] Write down only the figures and facts you need.
- [ ] Check the clock at about the halfway point.
- [ ] If a question has taken over 90 seconds, make your best choice and move on.
- [ ] Do not let a hard question rattle you. In an adaptive test, harder questions often mean you are doing well.

## Afterwards

- [ ] Write down which question types felt hardest while you still remember. It helps if you are retested later.
- [ ] Check your email for next steps. Some employers send a short report, many do not.
- [ ] If something went wrong technically, contact the employer or SHL's candidate support straight away with details.

## Last practice before the real one

If you have a day or more, one last timed run is the best use of an hour. The [free SHL Verify G+ practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/shl-general-ability?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=6cqf97&utm_content=shl-verify-g-plus) covers all three question types.
