# Timing and pacing

Verify G+ gives you 36 minutes for up to 30 questions. SHL's fact sheet gives an average testing time of 32 to 36 minutes, which tells you most people use nearly all of it. Pace is part of the test.

## The budget

| | |
|---|---|
| Time allowed | 36 minutes |
| Questions | Up to 30 |
| Average per question | About 72 seconds |
| SHL's average testing time | 32 to 36 minutes |

SHL does not publish a separate time limit for each ability, so plan for the test as a whole.

## A pacing plan for 36 minutes

This plan is our guidance, not an SHL rule.

| Question type | Target time | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Inductive | 45 to 60 seconds | Quick once the rule clicks. This is where you bank time. |
| Deductive, short "must be true" | 40 to 60 seconds | A few facts and five options |
| Deductive, ordering puzzle | Up to 2 minutes | Drawing the grid takes time |
| Numerical | 60 to 90 seconds | Finding the data and calculating |

Check the clock at about the halfway point. If you have answered around half the questions by 18 minutes, you are on pace.

## Where time usually goes

- **Re-reading tables.** Write down the two or three figures you need the first time.
- **Solving a whole ordering grid** when you only needed to check five options.
- **Searching for a second inductive rule** when one rule already explains every frame.
- **Exact calculation** when estimating would have ruled out four options.
- **Second-guessing.** Changing a careful answer on a hunch costs time and, more often than not, the mark.

## The adaptive format and pace

In an adaptive test the questions respond to your answers. Two practical points follow, and both are our guidance:

- **Do not panic if questions get harder.** In an adaptive design that usually means you are answering well.
- **Do not race the early questions.** Careless slips early on can steer you to easier questions that show less of what you can do.

## Practise against the clock

The notes teach the methods. Only timed practice teaches the pace. Do at least two full timed runs before the real test, such as the [free SHL Verify G+ practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/shl-general-ability?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=6cqf97&utm_content=shl-verify-g-plus), and note which question type pushed you over budget.
