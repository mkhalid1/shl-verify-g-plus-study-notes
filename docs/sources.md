## What SHL does not publish

We looked for these and did not find them on SHL's public pages, so the notes do not state them as fact:

- A pass mark or cut-off score
- Whether wrong answers are penalised
- The scoring model behind the adaptive test
- A time limit per ability
- Question counts or timings for the interactive version

## What is our own guidance

Methods, traps, pacing targets, study plans and every practice item are our own work. Where a page gives a time target or a tactic, it is our advice based on the published format, not an SHL rule.
