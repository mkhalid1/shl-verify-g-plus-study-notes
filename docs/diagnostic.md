# 15-minute diagnostic

Nine items, three for each ability. At the real test's pace of about 72 seconds an item, nine items take 11 minutes, so 15 minutes leaves you a little room to think. Set the timer anyway: a check without a clock tells you less. Have a calculator and paper ready, and answer everything before you open the answers. The items are our own, written for this repo, and are easier than the hardest questions you will meet in an adaptive test. Their job is to show which ability to study first, not to predict your score.

## Part A: Numerical

A café chain's three branches in one week.

| Branch | Coffees sold | Price per coffee |
|---|---|---|
| Harbour | 1,200 | £3.00 |
| Market | 900 | £3.40 |
| Station | 1,500 | £2.80 |

**A1.** Which branch took the most coffee revenue that week?

A. Harbour &nbsp; B. Market &nbsp; C. Station &nbsp; D. Harbour and Station equally &nbsp; E. Cannot say

**A2.** What was the chain's total coffee revenue that week?

A. £9,200 &nbsp; B. £10,080 &nbsp; C. £10,860 &nbsp; D. £11,520 &nbsp; E. £12,240

**A3.** Market raises its price by 10%, and the number of coffees it sells falls by 5%. What is Market's new weekly coffee revenue?

A. £2,907.00 &nbsp; B. £3,060.00 &nbsp; C. £3,197.70 &nbsp; D. £3,366.00 &nbsp; E. £3,519.00

## Part B: Deductive

**B1.** All forklift drivers at the depot hold a licence. Sam does not hold a licence. Which must be true?

A. Sam works at the depot. &nbsp; B. Sam is not a forklift driver at the depot. &nbsp; C. Sam is training for a licence. &nbsp; D. Everyone with a licence drives a forklift. &nbsp; E. Nothing can be concluded about Sam.

**B2.** The 9:05 train is faster than the 9:20. The 9:40 is slower than the 9:20. Which must be true?

A. The 9:20 is the fastest. &nbsp; B. The 9:40 is faster than the 9:05. &nbsp; C. The 9:05 is faster than the 9:40. &nbsp; D. All three take the same route. &nbsp; E. The 9:05 arrives first.

**B3.** "Our sales rose in the month after we redesigned the website, so the redesign caused the rise." Which, if true, most weakens this argument?

A. The redesign was done by an outside agency. &nbsp; B. Our main competitor closed the same month. &nbsp; C. The new site loads faster. &nbsp; D. The new site went live on a Monday. &nbsp; E. Customers liked the new colours.

## Part C: Inductive

**C1.** The number of dots in each frame: 1, 2, 4, 8. How many in the next frame?

A. 10 &nbsp; B. 12 &nbsp; C. 14 &nbsp; D. 16 &nbsp; E. 32

**C2.** A single arrow in each frame: ↑ ← ↓ →. What is the next frame?

A. ↑ &nbsp; B. → &nbsp; C. ↓ &nbsp; D. ← &nbsp; E. ↗

**C3.** A row of five cells with one shaded cell. What is the next frame?

| Frame 1 | Frame 2 | Frame 3 | Frame 4 | Frame 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ■□□□□ | □□■□□ | □□□□■ | □■□□□ | ? |

A. □□■□□ &nbsp; B. □□□■□ &nbsp; C. ■□□□□ &nbsp; D. □□□□■ &nbsp; E. □■□□□

## Answers

<details>
<summary>Answers and explanations</summary>

**A1: C, Station.** Revenues: Harbour 1,200 × £3.00 = £3,600, Market 900 × £3.40 = £3,060, Station 1,500 × £2.80 = £4,200.

**A2: C, £10,860.** £3,600 + £3,060 + £4,200.

**A3: C, £3,197.70.** New price £3.40 × 1.10 = £3.74. New volume 900 × 0.95 = 855. 855 × £3.74 = £3,197.70. *Why not D:* £3,366 applies the price rise and forgets the fall in volume.

**B1: B.** Every forklift driver at the depot holds a licence, so someone without one cannot be a forklift driver there. *Why not A:* Sam may not work at the depot at all.

**B2: C.** The 9:05 is faster than the 9:20, which is faster than the 9:40. *Why not E:* speed is not the same as arrival time, and the trains leave at different times.

**B3: B.** A competitor closing offers another reason for the rise, which breaks the link between the redesign and the sales. *Why not A, C, D or E:* none of them offers another explanation for the rise. A faster site (C) would, if anything, support the argument.

**C1: D, 16.** Each frame doubles: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16.

**C2: A, ↑.** The arrow turns 90° anticlockwise each frame: up, left, down, right, then up again.

**C3: B, □□□■□.** The shaded cell moves two cells right each frame and wraps around the end of the row: positions 1, 3, 5, then 2, then 4.

</details>

## What your result means

| Part | Score | Where to go |
|---|---|---|
| A: Numerical | 0 to 1 | Start with [numerical reasoning](sections/numerical-reasoning.md). It is usually the slowest section, so it needs the most practice. |
| B: Deductive | 0 to 1 | Start with [deductive reasoning](sections/deductive-reasoning.md) and learn the common forms table. |
| C: Inductive | 0 to 1 | Start with [inductive reasoning](sections/inductive-reasoning.md) and the feature-by-feature method. |
| Any part | 2 | Do that section's guide after your weakest one. |
| Any part | 3 | Solid. Skim its traps list and spend your time elsewhere. |

If you scored 3 on every part, go straight to a full timed run, because your main risk is pace. The [timing guide](timing.md) and the [free SHL Verify G+ practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/shl-general-ability?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=shl-verify-g-plus) are the next step.
